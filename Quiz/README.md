# 🎉 The Big Quiz Show

A bright, animated, kid-friendly quiz webpage built from **Themes Quiz (Classes 6–8)** — School Discipline, Waste Management, Personal & Community Hygiene, plus the Mixed Challenge — and **What If...? Situation Cards (Classes 6–8)**.

## Open it

Double-click **`index.html`** (Chrome or Edge works best). No installation or server is needed.
Or [CLICK HERE](https://campaign-psi-navy.vercel.app/)

## What's inside

- **3 theme quizzes** – 20 questions each (12 multiple choice, 6 true/false, 2 "What would you do?" discussion questions)
- **Mixed Challenge** – fill in the blanks, matching, and 6 odd-one-out questions
- **What If...?** – 30 situation cards (10 discipline, 10 waste, 10 hygiene) with three card types: 🔗 Chain Reaction, 🤔 Dilemma and 🌱 Be the Change
- **A fresh random order in every session** – questions and answer options reshuffle each time you play
- **74 marks total**, with the same score bands as the PDF (Champion / Great / Keep practising / Try again)
- **Instant feedback** with the explanation from the answer key
- **Stars, confetti, a bouncy mascot** and a rocket progress bar
- **Background music that changes with the section** – home, each quiz theme, results and What If...? each have their own tune. Sound effects too. Everything is generated in the browser with the Web Audio API, so there are no audio files to manage. Use the 🎵 and 🔊 buttons in the top bar to turn them off or on.
- **Read aloud** button on every question and situation card (browser speech)
- **Progress saved** on the device: best quiz scores out of 74 plus the What If cards you have explored, with a report card and a reset button
- Works on phones, tablets, laptops and classroom projectors

## What If...? cards

Open the purple **What If...?** panel on the home page. Each card follows the **Stop · Think · Choose** routine:

1. **Stop** – What exactly is happening?
2. **Think** – Who is affected? What could happen next?
3. **Choose** – What is the kindest, fairest and most responsible action?

Tap **Reveal thinking prompts** for discussion starters and a kind idea. Use the theme filters, **Shuffle** button or the ← → arrow keys to move through the deck. Cards are shuffled in a new order each session.

## Notes

- Browsers block audio until the first tap/click. Music starts automatically after the first interaction (or first quiz card tap).
- Best scores and explored cards live in the browser's local storage. Use **Reset my progress** on the home page to clear them for a new student.
- Quiz content comes from `Themes_Quiz_Classes_6-8.pdf`; situation cards come from `What_If_Situation_Cards_Classes_6-8.pdf`.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts when online; the page still works offline with a rounded fallback font.
